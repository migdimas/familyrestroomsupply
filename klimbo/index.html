Featured brand · Child step stools

Klimbo. Every child reaches the sink.

Children aged 2 to 8 can’t reach a standard sink. Klimbo gives them their own step: the child pulls it out with a foot, steps up, washes, and the step folds back after each use. No more lifting a toddler over the counter.

01

Kids wash on their own

At the same sinks as everyone else, without a parent lifting them.

02

Folds back after each use

K01 and K02 retract on their own, so the step is never left out as a tripping hazard.

03

Built for public use

Aluminum or steel, Axalta powder coat, non slip tread made from recycled tires.

04

Quick install

Anchored to the concrete slab in front of an existing sink. No plumbing work.

Where it goes: Klimbo is meant for restrooms with more than one sink. Equip one sink in the row; final placement is confirmed by the project’s design professional.
