Markets / Theme parks and attractions

Family restrooms for theme parks and attractions.

In a park, the restroom is part of the ride. Families with strollers, toddlers and grandparents need rooms that work at peak volume, all day, every day.

What facility teams tell us

  • Peak crowds and long lines at family rooms
  • Toddlers who can’t reach the sinks
  • Guests caring for an adult with nowhere to change
  • Finishes that must survive heavy cleaning

Typical family restroom package

  • Klimbo retractable step stools on one sink per row
  • Baby changing stations in men’s, women’s and family rooms
  • Adult changing tables in universal rooms
  • Heavy duty partitions, grab bars and accessories
  • Family restroom signage and wayfinding

How we work with you

  • Standard package per restroom type, repeatable across the park
  • Submittals for your design and construction teams
  • Coordinated delivery with your contractor’s schedule
