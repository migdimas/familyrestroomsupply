Why it matters

The sink is the one fixture kids can’t use.

In most public restrooms, a parent has to lift a child to the faucet with one arm, often while holding a bag or a younger sibling. Families notice, and they talk about the places that thought of them.

Featured brand

Klimbo. Every child reaches the sink.

Children aged 2 to 8 can’t reach a standard sink. Klimbo gives them their own step: the child pulls it out with a foot, steps up, washes, and the step tucks away again after each use. No more lifting a toddler over the counter.

01

Universal access

Kids wash their hands on their own, at the same sinks as everyone else.

02

Retracts after each use

K01 and K02 fold back on their own, so the step is never left out as a tripping hazard.

03

Built for public use

Aluminum or steel, Axalta powder coat, non slip tread made from recycled tires.

04

Fast install

Anchored to the concrete slab in front of an existing sink. No plumbing work.
