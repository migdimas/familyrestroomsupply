Why it matters

Families notice the restroom.

Parents lifting toddlers to the faucet, a dad with no changing station, an adult caregiver with nowhere to help. Family friendly restrooms are now part of the guest experience, and in a growing number of codes and laws they are part of the requirement.

  • Guest experienceA family restroom is one of the first things parents look for in a park, a restaurant or a terminal.
  • Codes and lawsBuilding codes and federal and state laws increasingly call for family rooms and changing stations.
  • Brand consistencyChains want the same family package in every location, specified once and rolled out everywhere.
